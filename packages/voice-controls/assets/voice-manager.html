Codex voice volume

Back to Codex Desktop Toolkit

During a voice call, a volume slider and Auto checkbox appear below the orb. Auto lowers the assistant's volume after 200 ms of sustained sound and restores it after a 3-second pause. Drag the slider for manual control; select Auto again to resume ducking. This controls only the assistant's playback volume for the current call; system volume and cloud generation are unchanged.
